What grid tracking measures

Rank on Google Maps is not one number. Where you show up in the map pack changes depending on where the searcher is standing. So instead of checking rank from a single point, we scan a grid of points around the location and record your position at every one. Three numbers come out of that scan.

  • ARP: average position across the points where you show up.
  • ATRP: average position across every point in the grid, counting the points where you do not show up as misses.
  • SoLV: the percentage of the area where you land in the top 3.
