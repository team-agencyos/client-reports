Lean & Supply Chain ConsultingThe Assessment

Find the few problems that cause most of the pain. Then fix those first.

Most leaders can name the symptoms: productivity is off, inventory is too high, orders ship late. The hard part is knowing which problems matter most, what is causing them, and what to fix first. That is what the Assessment answers, and why more than 90% of our engagements start here.

How it works

Two days on site. A roadmap in two to three weeks.

  • Two days on site, three for multi-channel or multi-site operations. We interview the people closest to the work, walk the floor, and review your operating data.
  • 80/20, not a mega-study. We look for the small number of issues driving most of your operational cost and delay.
  • Your team is in the room, so the findings are theirs too, not a consultant's report that sits on a shelf.
  • Read-out on the last day on site, then a written Assessment Report and an executive presentation with Q&A, typically two to three weeks later.
Deliverables

What you get

  • Prioritized improvement opportunities, including quick wins you can start on immediately.
  • Recommended projects, each with objectives, the tools we would use, scope, timing, the consulting fee, and the return on investment where the data supports it.
  • A vision of the future state and the longer-term strategic initiatives behind it.
  • Nominated implementation team and timing. You decide what to run, in what order, and whether to run it with us or on your own.
Before we arrive

What we need from you

A short data package, which most clients already have: roughly 12 months of demand and purchase history at SKU level, current inventory, any performance measures you already track, an org chart and a facility drawing. Getting it to us ahead of time is what keeps the Assessment to two days.
