Why we start here

Every organization has a list of problems. Knowing where to begin is the harder question.

Productivity is off, inventory is too high, orders are late, the warehouse is out of space, throughput has plateaued. Most leaders can name the symptoms. Far fewer know which one is actually costing them the most, or what is causing it.

We do not start by selling projects. Unlike firms that run a discovery meeting and then propose a predetermined solution, we learn your business and your people first. Only then do we recommend improvement projects, each with its scope, objectives, tools, timing, fee, and the return on investment where the data supports it.

How it works

Two days on site. A roadmap in two to three weeks.

Before we arrive

What we need from you

To keep the Assessment to two days, we ask for a short data package before we arrive. Most clients already have all of it.

  • Roughly 12 months of demand and purchase history at SKU level
  • Current inventory levels
  • Any performance measures and goals you already track
  • An org chart and a facility drawing

What we find

The Assessment confirms what you suspect. Its value is what sits underneath.

  • Inventory problems caused by planning issues
  • Production delays created by poor material flow or supplier quality
  • Warehouse congestion driven by the wrong storage media
  • Customer service issues that originate in the ERP configuration
  • Excess labor resulting from inefficient process design

Without seeing these relationships, companies spend significant time and money solving the wrong problem.

Common questions

Before you ask

How long does an Assessment take?

Two days on site for most operations, three for multi-channel or multi-site, with the written report and executive presentation typically two to three weeks later.

We already know what our problems are.

Usually the symptoms, yes. The Assessment identifies the root causes and the sequence to address them, which is where improvement efforts most often fail.

Is the Assessment just a sales tool for projects?

No predetermined solution walks in the door. Every recommended project comes with scope, fee and expected return so you can decide, and you can implement any of them without us.

We are not a manufacturer. Does this apply to us?

Assessments run in distribution and warehousing, retail, food and beverage, healthcare supply chains and business services, not only manufacturing.

How much of our team's time does it take?

Interviews and a facility tour across two days, scheduled around the operation, plus the data package before we arrive.

What happens after the report?

You choose. You keep the report, the roadmap and the quick wins either way.

How is this different from a large consulting firm's assessment?

Weeks-long, team-heavy studies are a different product. We apply the 80/20 principle with senior consultants who then run the projects themselves.

What does an Assessment cost?

The fee depends on scope, the number of facilities and the depth of analysis required, and is fixed in the scope-of-work before we begin. Travel is invoiced at cost.
