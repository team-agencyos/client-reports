Where the notifications go. Each submission sends one email from sarah@supplyvelocity.com to mitch@, stacy@, ray@, zohar@ and sarah@supplyvelocity.com with the subject "New Request for Consultation submission" or "New Hero Consultation Request submission". The delivery log covers the last 15 days: every email was accepted by four of the five mailboxes, and stacy@supplyvelocity.com has returned a temporary delivery failure on every email since at least September 15. "Accepted" means the mail server took the message; if it is not in the inbox, it is in spam or Promotions.

Search: clicks held, impressions fell

Search Console, weekly, Jul 27 to Sep 27
Impressions fell by two thirds since late July while clicks stayed in the same band. The impressions that disappeared were on broad terms such as "supply chain consulting" and "supply chain management consulting", where the site ranked on page four or five and almost never earned a click. Over the same weeks "lean manufacturing consultant" moved from around position 20 to the top ten and "inventory optimization consulting" from 41 to 16. Google ran a spam update August 18 to 21 and began another on September 24; neither targets a site like this one, and no core update was confirmed in September.

Other signals checked

Visits to the contact page

Higher in the second half of September (61 views) than the first (49). People were still reaching the form.

Visits from search, United States

47 to 62 a week through September, level with August. September 2026 is well above September 2025 on every measure, so this is not a seasonal dip.

Visits from AI assistants

44 in August, 18 in September. This is the channel that produced the private-equity enquiries earlier in the year, and the AI visibility scan is being re-run to see whether the engines still name Supply Velocity as they did in early September.

The website itself

No change to the form submission code since before August. The only September change was an optional "How did you find us?" field on September 8, and submissions continued after it.

What we are doing

This week. Re-running the AI visibility scan and sending the result. Moving the notifications to a dedicated, fully authenticated sending address with a copy to Outrider Digital, so a missed email is caught on our side. Flagging stacy@supplyvelocity.com for someone at Supply Velocity to check.

If you want it. A sorting rule that separates vendor pitches from genuine enquiries before they reach your inbox. In July you preferred to keep the form open rather than risk losing a real lead; this would sort, not block.
