Organic traffic is the part of your visits that comes from people finding you on Google, so it is the number the content and link work is meant to move. August came in slightly below July, and we know exactly where: two of your older blog posts, the boutique consulting firms guide and the warehouse bin location guide, each slipped a few positions on Google. They are your two most visited pages from search, so a small slip there shows up in the monthly total. Everything else held steady, including searches for Supply Velocity by name. August is also a quieter month for business search every year. We are refreshing both posts for 2026 with stronger titles and a clearer answer at the top, which is the quickest way to win those clicks back because Google is already showing the pages tens of thousands of times a month. Alongside that, the six new pages are indexing and the three August backlinks are live, so we expect the numbers to firm up over September and October.
