The first full month of search data since launch, and a strong start for a four-week-old site. Google sent 47 visitors in the four weeks to September 28, up by more than a third on the four weeks before, from 466 appearances in results. One in ten people who saw the site clicked it, which is well above the three to five percent that counts as healthy, and the site holds an average position just inside the top ten. The searches bringing people in are the right ones: your studio name first, then pilates near me, pilates Saline and pilates Ann Arbor. The local pages are starting to appear for find pilates classes near me and ann arbor pilates studio without earning clicks yet, which is normal in their first weeks. Search Console reports two to three days behind, so the window runs to September 28.
