⚠ Publish blocker found during the crawl

Every new URL in this plan currently returns a 301 → homepage (checked Aug 10, 2026). A catch-all redirect is swallowing the new paths — e.g. /commercial-cleaning-austin/ and /blog/commercial-cleaning-checklist/ both bounce to /. Those catch-alls must be removed as each page publishes, or Google will keep seeing redirects instead of the new content.

Also: the page sitemap's lastmod is Jul 5 while content was finished Jul 14–24 — worth confirming Rank Math regenerates the sitemap when the new pages go live.
