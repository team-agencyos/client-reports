Content Brain checks

No young adults (Brain §2, §8.7 — intake rule)

  • Every primary and secondary keyword was scanned for young-adult / adult / 18+ / college-student / transitional-age terms. Hits: 0.

  • No page in this plan targets anyone outside ages 13–17.

  • Flag for approval: the aftercare page's secondaries teen transitional living program and teen sober living are teen-modified, but transitional living and sober living programs are commonly 18+. Brain §2 bans "transitional age youth". Recommend keeping them only if the page states ages 13–17 explicitly, or dropping them. Not changed here — needs a decision.

  • The young-adult blog content is KEY-39, not this item.

No fear-mongering (Brain §5 named rule, §8.11)

  • Keyword scan for fear hooks and Brain-banned phrases. Hits: 1 page — Programs for troubled teens (boot camp, troubled teen).

  • Programs for troubled teens needs approval before it is built. KEY-16 recommends it (1,600/mo, KD 20) and says to position it as licensed clinical care, the opposite of a boot camp. But the Content Brain, updated after KEY-16, lists "troubled teen industry" and "boot camp" under Never Use, and flags the homepage's "troubled teen" phrasing for removal. Parents searching these terms are often frightened, so the page is also the highest fear-mongering risk in the plan. It stays in the plan as KEY-16 specifies; it should not be drafted until Jack or the client rules on whether the keyword may be used in title/meta/URL (the way Brain §5 allows "rehab" and "substance abuse").

  • Crisis-adjacent pages (suicidaleation, self-harm, opioids) are mapped to plain clinical terms. Suicidaleation is explicitly not optimised for traffic. 988 must be present on all three (Brain §6).

  • No urgency, scarcity or "beds" framing appears in any target keyword.

Other Brain rules touched by the plan

  • No false locations (Brain §6): no city pages. The only local page is the LA outpatient page, which names the real facility and stays inside the ~15 mi radius. Residential pages target national terms; Malibu stays a qualifier, never a luxury signal.

  • "Rehab" / "substance abuse": title, meta and URL only, never body copy (Brain §5).

  • Mental health first: substance pages keep the mental-health driver in scope; hard-drug pages are not prioritised (Brain §8.24).
